Frequently asked questions

Short answers about orders, products and partnership

Ordering

How can I place an order?

There are four ways: the online order page, phone (+998 95 342 70 70), the Telegram button in the corner of the site, or our sales agent. After you order, our manager will call to confirm.

Where can I see prices?

Prices are shown on the online order page. The manager confirms the final price, discounts and delivery terms when confirming your order.

How do delivery and payment work?

We deliver through sales agents and distributors across Tashkent. Delivery time and payment method are agreed with the manager depending on order size and address.

Can I buy directly for home use?

Yes. Place an order on the online order page as a “Private customer” — there are 200–500 g packs for home. The manager will explain delivery terms.

How do I find the sales agent for my area?

Call our sales department (+998 95 342 70 70) or send a request — the agent serving your district will contact you.

Can I try a product first?

Yes. Click “Get a free sample” on the margarine, Bulut or HoReCa pages — our manager will contact you to arrange delivery of the sample.

Products

Which margarine is best for samsa and puff pastry?

For home baking — Margaritto Pastry 80% (200 g / 500 g). For samsa shops and production — Margaritto 80% for puff pastry (10 kg, 2 kg sheets). Both give clear layers and good volume.

Which margarine suits creams?

Margaritto 72% for creams or, if you need more volume and stability, Margaritto 80% premium. For large orders the creamy flavour can be replaced with ice-cream flavour (minimum order — 1000 kg).

What is the difference between margarine and spread?

Margaritto margarines are made for baking and confectionery. Smaylo and Creamy Breakfast are vegetable-cream spreads — a worthy alternative to butter for breakfast, sandwiches and cooking. More in the catalogue.

Are the products certified?

Yes. Our production follows ISO 22000:2018 and HACCP, and our products hold Halal, conformity and sanitary-epidemiological certificates.

What are Bulut products made from?

Napkins, paper towels and toilet paper are 100% cellulose; wet wipes are made of viscose. We produce the paper base ourselves.

Where can I download the product catalogue?

PDF catalogues (in Russian and English) are on the Production page.

Which country is Aberno from?

Aberno Group is an Uzbek company. Since 2021 we have been making Margaritto, Smaylo and Bulut products at our own plant in Tashkent.

Partnership

How can I become a distributor?

Send a request on the Sales department page. Distributors get special prices, the full range and a personal manager.

Is there a special offer for cafés or restaurants?

Yes — the HoReCa page brings together dispenser napkins, towels and wholesale margarines. Use the form there to get an offer for your venue.

Do you sell raw materials for napkin production?

Yes. Describe your needs in the form on the Raw materials page — we will provide a sample for testing.

Can you make napkins with our logo?

Send a request for branded napkins for your venue via the HoReCa form — our manager will check feasibility, minimum volume and lead times and get back to you.

Do you export to other countries?

We review export requests individually. Tell us the country, products and approximate volume and contact us.

Can we visit your plant?

Visits for partnership purposes are arranged in advance — contact us.
