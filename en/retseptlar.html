Puff pastry samsa

Golden samsa with clearly separated layers. A dedicated puff pastry margarine makes the dough even and airy.

  • ⏱ 2.5 hours
  • 🍽 12 pcs
  • 🔥 200°C

Ingredients

  • Flour — 500 g
  • Water — 250 ml
  • Salt — 1 tsp
  • Margaritto Pastry 80% — 200 g
  • Beef — 500 g
  • Onions — 3
  • Cumin, black pepper, salt — to taste
  • Egg yolk and sesame seeds — for the top

Processing

  1. Knead a firm dough from flour, water and salt and let it rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Roll the dough out thinly, spread with softened margarine and roll it up. Chill for 1 hour.
  3. Finely chop the meat and onions, add spices and salt and mix.
  4. Cut the roll into 12 pieces, roll out each one, add the filling and fold into a triangle.
  5. Brush with egg yolk, sprinkle with sesame and bake at 200°C for 30–35 minutes.

Napoleon cake

Crisp flaky layers and a delicate creamy filling. Puff pastry margarine for the layers, cream margarine for the filling.

  • ⏱ 2 hours + 8 hours resting
  • 🍽 10–12 slices
  • 🔥 200°C

Ingredients

  • For the layers:
  • Flour — 400 g
  • Margaritto Pastry 80% (cold) — 250 g
  • Egg — 1
  • Cold water — 120 ml
  • Vinegar — 1 tbsp, salt — a pinch
  • For the cream:
  • Milk — 700 ml
  • Sugar — 200 g
  • Eggs — 2, flour — 50 g, vanilla
  • Margaritto 72% for creams — 150 g

Processing

  1. Chop the cold margarine into the flour with a knife until it forms fine crumbs.
  2. Add the egg, water, vinegar and salt, quickly knead the dough, divide into 8 pieces and chill for 1 hour.
  3. Roll each piece out thinly, prick with a fork and bake at 200°C for 7–8 minutes.
  4. Cook the milk, sugar, eggs, flour and vanilla over low heat until thick, then cool. Whip with the margarine.
  5. Spread cream on the layers, stack them, sprinkle with crumbs and leave for 8 hours.

Croissant

Croissants that are airy inside and crisp outside. The 2 kg sheets are convenient for laminating dough.

  • ⏱ 5 hours
  • 🍽 12 pcs
  • 🔥 200°C

Ingredients

  • Flour — 500 g
  • Milk — 250 ml
  • Sugar — 60 g
  • Dry yeast — 10 g
  • Salt — 10 g
  • Egg — 1 (+1 for brushing)
  • Margaritto 80% for puff pastry — 250 g

Processing

  1. Make a dough from flour, milk, sugar, yeast, salt and egg and chill for 1 hour.
  2. Shape the margarine into a 15×15 cm sheet and wrap it in the rolled-out dough.
  3. Roll the dough out lengthwise and fold it in three. Repeat 3 times, chilling for 30 minutes each time.
  4. Roll the dough to 4 mm, cut into triangles and roll them up from the wide end.
  5. Leave to rise in a warm place for 1.5–2 hours, brush with egg and bake at 200°C for 15–18 minutes.

Chocolate muffins

Soft, moist muffins. Smaylo spread gives the bake a creamy taste.

  • ⏱ 40 minutes
  • 🍽 12 pcs
  • 🔥 180°C

Ingredients

  • Flour — 250 g
  • Sugar — 150 g
  • Smaylo spread (melted) — 120 g
  • Eggs — 2
  • Milk — 200 ml
  • Cocoa — 30 g
  • Baking powder — 10 g, salt — a pinch
  • Chocolate chunks — 100 g

Processing

  1. Mix the flour, cocoa, baking powder and salt.
  2. In another bowl mix the eggs, sugar, milk and melted spread.
  3. Combine both mixtures, add the chocolate and stir lightly — the batter doesn’t need to be smooth.
  4. Fill the moulds two-thirds full.
  5. Bake at 180°C for 20–25 minutes.

Milk buns

Soft, fluffy buns for breakfast and tea. All-purpose margarine helps the dough rise well.

  • ⏱ 2.5 hours
  • 🍽 12 pcs
  • 🔥 180°C

Ingredients

  • Flour — 500 g
  • Warm milk — 250 ml
  • Sugar — 70 g
  • Egg — 1 (+1 for brushing)
  • Dry yeast — 7 g
  • Salt — 1 tsp
  • Margaritto Universal 80% (softened) — 70 g

Processing

  1. Dissolve the yeast and sugar in warm milk and wait 10 minutes.
  2. Add the flour, egg, salt and margarine and knead a soft dough for 10 minutes.
  3. Cover the dough and leave to rise in a warm place for 1 hour.
  4. Shape 12 round buns and let them rise for another 40 minutes.
  5. Brush with egg and bake at 180°C for 20–25 minutes.

Chebureks

Crispy chebureks with small bubbles on the surface and a juicy filling. Margarine in the dough makes it soft and elastic.

  • ⏱ 1.5 hours
  • 🍽 10 pcs
  • 🔥 frying

Ingredients

  • For the dough:
  • Flour — 500 g
  • Water — 200 ml
  • Egg — 1
  • Margaritto Universal 80% (melted) — 60 g
  • Salt — 1 tsp
  • For the filling:
  • Minced beef or lamb — 400 g
  • Onions — 2
  • Cold water — 50 ml
  • Salt, black pepper, cumin — to taste
  • Vegetable oil for frying

Processing

  1. Mix the water, egg, salt and melted margarine, gradually add the flour and knead a firm dough. Let it rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Finely chop the onion, add to the mince with the cold water and spices and mix well — the filling will be juicy.
  3. Divide the dough into 10 pieces and roll each into a thin circle.
  4. Place filling on half of the circle, fold over the other half and press the edges with a fork.
  5. Fry in hot oil for 2–3 minutes on each side until golden.

Walnut heart buns

Soft heart-shaped buns with sugar and walnuts — loved by children and adults alike.

  • ⏱ 2 hours
  • 🍽 12 pcs
  • 🔥 180°C

Ingredients

  • Flour — 500 g
  • Warm milk — 250 ml
  • Dry yeast — 7 g
  • Sugar — 60 g (+80 g for sprinkling)
  • Egg — 1 (+1 for brushing)
  • Margaritto Universal 80% (softened) — 80 g (+30 g for brushing)
  • Chopped walnuts — 100 g
  • Salt — a pinch

Processing

  1. Dissolve the yeast and sugar in warm milk and leave for 10 minutes until foamy.
  2. Add the flour, egg, salt and softened margarine, knead a soft dough, cover and leave to rise for 1 hour.
  3. Roll the dough into a rectangle, brush with melted margarine and sprinkle with sugar and walnuts.
  4. Roll it up along the long side and cut into 12 pieces. Cut each piece lengthwise halfway and open the halves into a heart shape.
  5. Leave to rise for 15 minutes, brush with egg and bake at 180°C for 20–25 minutes until golden.
