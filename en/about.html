About us in brief

Aberno Butters Group has been producing fat and oil products since 2021. Today our portfolio includes up to 30 products, and we are constantly developing new ones to expand the range.

Besides margarines and spreads, the company makes a variety of napkins and paper products under the Bulut brand and produces raw materials for napkin manufacturers. We deliver through our own sales department, a team of distributors and sales agents across Tashkent.
