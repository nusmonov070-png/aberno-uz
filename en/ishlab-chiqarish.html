Margarines and spreads

How Margaritto and Smaylo are made

Aberno Butters Group has been producing margarines and spreads at its own plant since 2021. Our lines can produce up to 2000 kg per hour; current actual output is 520 tonnes per month (1300 kg per hour).

  • 200–500 g bars for home and 2.5–20 kg packs for production
  • Special margarines for confectionery, puff pastry, creams and bakeries
  • Every batch is controlled at all stages of production
  • Taste can be customised for large orders (e.g. ice-cream flavour)

Bulut

Paper products — from our own raw materials

Bulut napkins, paper towels and toilet paper are made from 100% cellulose on our own lines. We make the paper base ourselves, so quality is consistent.

Building on this experience, we also supply raw materials to other napkin manufacturers.
