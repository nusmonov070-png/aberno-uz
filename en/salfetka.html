About Bulut

Just like its name — like a cloud

Bulut is Aberno Group’s paper hygiene brand. From napkins to kitchen towels, all our products share one idea: a home full of softness and cleanliness.

Modern lines, quality cellulose and careful control — like its name, Bulut brings lightness to every home.

  • 100% cellulose — soft and strong
  • Our own raw materials and our own production
  • Products for home, office, car and HoReCa
  • Delivery through sales agents across Tashkent
